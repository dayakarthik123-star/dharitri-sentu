# Future Pilot and Validation Plan

> **Important Distinction**: This document defines a **proposed future validation plan**. No field pilot, user study, or departmental trial has been conducted yet.

## 1. Proposed Participant Groups (Target: 30–45 Participants)
1. **Prospective Land Buyers (15 participants)**: First-time urban and semi-urban buyers in Kamrup and Dibrugarh evaluating residential or agricultural plots.
2. **Landholders / Sellers (15 participants)**: Smallholder agricultural and homestead owners familiar with paper Patta/Jamabandi extracts.
3. **Legal / Documentation Reviewers (5–10 participants)**: Advocates, deed writers, and paralegal reviewers experienced in Assam land due diligence.

## 2. Evaluation Methodology & Success Measures
- **Task Completion Rate**: Percentage of participants who can locate a parcel on the map, open its Land Trust Passport, and correctly identify a planted discrepancy (e.g., `2.50 Bigha` vs `2.35 Bigha`) within 3 minutes.
- **Disclaimer Comprehension Score**: Percentage of users who accurately understand that a Land Trust Passport is an evidence summary and **not** an official government title certificate.
- **Trilingual Accessibility**: Comprehension and task speed across Assamese (`as`), Hindi (`hi`), and English (`en`) interfaces on 360px–390px mobile screens.

## 3. Pre-Conditions for Any Authorised Departmental Pilot
- Written departmental authorisation and MoU specifying read-only sandbox API scope.
- Independent CERT-In empanelled security audit and VAPT clearance.
- Informed participant consent and institutional ethics approval.
